Reconcile

Amount + date matching, straight from your worksheets.

Click the row that holds each sheet's column names — that's all the tool needs to know about your format. Untick anything you don't want to reconcile.

Drag from a column on one table to a column on another to compare them. Draw more than one line between the same two tables and a row has to agree on all of them. Click a line to change how it's compared.

Include at least two sheets on step 1 to draw a comparison.

One block per pair of sheets. Click a row number to open that sheet in the Data view.
